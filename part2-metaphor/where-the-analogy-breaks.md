# Where the analogy breaks

> Status: DRAFT. Your words, seams merged. Short by design, around 350 words. No
> dashes. This file is load-bearing: it is what keeps the repo from reading as a
> gimmick. Revise in place.

Machine learning is the vehicle in this repo, not the subject. The vehicle has
limits, and naming them is part of the work.

1. **ML has no native substitution operation.**
   Notebook C builds the transfer by hand. It is a for loop moving entries from
   one list to another. Nothing in how a model trains does this on its own.
   Substitution is not a property of learning systems, we staged it. What the
   staged version is good for is seeing the shape of the operation, and through
   that shape catching even a sliver of the love Christ has given His creation.

2. **A cost is not a suffering.**
   A number in a cost matrix does not bleed. The whole weight of the atonement is
   that a person, not an abstraction, bore it willingly. Every notebook here
   trades in scalars, and a scalar is finite. What Christ was willing to pay is
   not. That gap is the entire distance the metaphor cannot cross.

3. **The debt in the gospel is relational, not numeric.**
   Ledger is a useful image for conservation. It is a bad image for what sin
   actually breaks, which is a relationship, not a balance sheet. The point of
   the payment was the chance to be reconciled to God, to have that relationship
   again despite our sinful nature. Do not let the accounting frame flatten that.

4. **Model B did not choose.**
   In Notebook C, Model B absorbs the debt because the code says so. The
   substitution at the center of the faith is an act of will and love, and
   coercion would make it a different thing entirely. Model B had no choice. We
   still do: to trust what Jesus's sacrifice did, and through it have that
   relationship with God.

5. **Optimization has no telos beyond the loss.**
   A model minimizes what you tell it to minimize. It has no account of what a
   person is for. A machine can never understand the relational weight the
   crucifixion carries. Part 4 is about the thing no objective function reaches.

## The honest summary line

The math shows the shape of what grace and justice served for us and for God. It
cannot show the repair of the relationship itself.
